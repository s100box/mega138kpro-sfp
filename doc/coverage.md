## Hole triage

Both modules reach 100% line and branch coverage. The full-peer bench checks
key reset, unmapped writes/readback, each SerDes reset selection, both lanes'
word-order controls and TX_OFF, repeated and lane-specific injection, lock loss
and recovery, and FIFO backpressure with a pending injection. The lane bench
retains its independent serial-bit PRBS oracle.

Counter saturation is tested by seeding only counter state near the boundary
at a receive-clock edge, then sending real errors through the complete checker.
The tests require saturating error-bit, error-word and lost-lock counts to stay
at their maxima on later errors, and require the 64-bit locked-word count to
wrap. This checks the boundary arithmetic without billions of events; it does
not establish every possible counter-state transition.

Remaining toggle holes are retained in the denominator:

- Constant outputs and reserved fields: both SFP TX-disable pins are tied low;
  unused STATUS and LANE_CFG bits are tied zero.
- The full-peer UART bench does not carry large error-counter values into all
  register-result bits. High locked-word snapshot bits are not observed at
  both polarities; the bounded lane-level seed checks wrap and arithmetic,
  rather than every snapshot transport bit.
- The benchmark uses aligned register addresses and a small frequency gate,
  leaving high address bits, low alignment bits and large frequency-result
  bits unchanged. Lane 1 FIFO backpressure is not independently toggled in
  the full-peer stand-in; the common lane implementation is exercised by the
  stalled lane 0 case and its dedicated lane bench.

Run `tools/cov_summary.py --holes` for the point-level list. Behavioral SerDes
word ordering and reset-pin selection do not qualify vendor analog behavior,
physical reference-clock routing or operation at a requested line rate. Those
remain hardware checks described in the design.
